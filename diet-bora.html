2. DIET BORA PROGRAM

Turning Nutrition Challenges into Practical Community Solutions

Diet Bora is Jamii Ajibika's flagship program. The Diet Bora Program promotes nutrition-smart livelihoods, food security, climate resilience and economic empowerment, particularly among youth, caregivers and vulnerable communities.

What if nutrition solutions could also create livelihoods?

Instead of treating nutrition, agriculture, youth unemployment and climate resilience as separate issues, Diet Bora brings them together.

DIET BORA'S KEY QUESTIONS

Diet Bora asks:

Can a young person learn poultry farming and build an income-generating enterprise?

Can locally available resources help reduce feed costs?

Can cricket and azolla contribute to alternative protein and feed systems?

Can agricultural waste and local resources become productive inputs rather than waste?

Can nutrition education be connected to actual food production?

Can young mothers become entrepreneurs rather than permanent recipients of assistance?

Can a community demonstration site become a classroom, incubation centre and innovation laboratory?

Can nutrition advocacy influence both household behaviour and public policy?

These questions drive the practical work of Diet Bora.
