OUR APPROACH

We are community-led, practical and solution-oriented.

01

Listen

We begin by understanding the realities, experiences, priorities and aspirations of communities.

02

Mobilize

We bring together youth, women, caregivers, community leaders, government actors, technical experts and partners around shared challenges.

03

Build Capacity

We provide training, mentorship, civic education, enterprise development and practical skills.

04

Demonstrate

We believe communities learn best when solutions can be seen, tested and practiced.

05

Incubate

We move beyond training by helping participants translate knowledge into enterprise opportunities.

06

Connect

We facilitate linkages with markets, institutions, technical partners, financing opportunities and relevant government structures.

07

Advocate

We create platforms through which community members can participate in public decision-making and influence policies and resource allocation.

08

Document & Learn

We document experiences, results, challenges and lessons so that successful approaches can be improved and replicated.

This is why Jamii Ajibika is developing practical learning and innovation spaces around poultry, cricket, azolla and feed innovation.

Our implementation framework emphasizes community-based enterprise incubation, training and mentorship, collaboration with institutions and digital advocacy, supported by participatory monitoring and learning.
